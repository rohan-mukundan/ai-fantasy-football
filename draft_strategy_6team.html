6-Team Rules — From the Data
🏆
Pure BPA through round 5. No positional scarcity exists in a 6-team league. The player who finishes #1 at their position is almost always available — draft the best player, period.
⚡
Exploit the waiver wire aggressively. 910 players available. PFR data: ~1.2 undrafted/late RBs finish top-30 every year (Robinson 2020, Pollard 2022, Conner 2021). In a 6-team league, these players are untouched on waivers.
⚠️
Never reach for QB before round 6. QBs finishing top-5 at ADP rounds 1–3 are a trap — data shows ~1 QB per year busts at that ADP. With 14 QBs on waivers, you lose nothing by waiting.
🎯
If Kelce or #1 TE falls to round 3, take him. Top-2 TEs averaged top-8 overall finishes from 2020–2024. In a 6-team league they may slip past round 2. If available at 3, the value is too good to pass.
🔄
Stream K and DEF every single week. 26 kickers and 26 defenses on waivers. Matchup-stream DEF — PFR data shows top-5 DEF units change year to year unpredictably. Rostering a second DEF wastes a bench spot.
📊
WR value over RB depth. PFR 2020–24 shows WRs average 3.5 top-12 overall finishes per year vs 4 for RBs. But WR bust rate at top-10 ADP is lower (1–2/yr) than RB (3/yr). In a 6-team league, grab elite WRs early without fear.
